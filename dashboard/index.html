How the value is worked out

implied value = TVL × sector multiple × growth factor × maturity factor × breadth factor

The sector multiple is the market's own verdict, read off this snapshot: the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the live-token protocols in the same category, at least three of them, clamped to 0.05–1.50. A category with fewer than three live comparables, or none at all, falls back to the global median. Growth is the 7-day TVL change clamped to −60%…+150%, counted at half its value. Maturity runs from 0.6 for a protocol listed yesterday to 1.0 after three years; a protocol with no listing date is scored as one year old. Breadth adds 4% per extra chain, up to five.
